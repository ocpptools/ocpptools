Add the OCPP 1.6J and OCPP 2.0.1 JSON Schemas as byte-for-byte copies of the files published by the Open Charge Alliance, which is their author and copyright holder, under CC BY-ND 4.0. The package ships the license text, a manifest with the SHA-256 of every file, and the sources of the copies.
