First public release of `@ocpptools/schema-utils` and `@ocpptools/validator` at 0.2.0, in the fixed version group with `@ocpptools/core` 0.2.0. The validator validates OCPP 1.6J and 2.0.1 frames and payloads with a lazy schema registry, Ajv, and readable issues. The schema utilities provide draft normalization, `$ref` resolution, field metadata, and payload defaults.
